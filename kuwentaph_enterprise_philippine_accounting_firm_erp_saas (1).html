All ERP Modules & Sub-Modules Under KuwentaPH

Every module is engineered specifically around Philippine National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC) rules, BIR Computerized Accounting System (CAS) standards, and multi-client firm workflows.

1. Multi-Tenant Client & Entity Hub

Centralized practice management for CPA firms handling multiple corporations and sole proprietors.

  • 14-Digit TIN, RDO Code & ORUS Registration Tracking
  • VAT (12%), Zero-Rated (PEZA/BOI) & Non-VAT (3%) Regimes
  • Industry-Specific Chart of Accounts (COA) Provisioning
  • Cross-Client Compliance & Filing Status Matrix

2. Bookkeeping & BIR Books of Accounts

Real-time double-entry accounting engine replacing manual columnar books and spreadsheets.

  • Journal Voucher Encoder with strict Debit = Credit check
  • 6 Statutory Books: General Journal, GL, CRB, CDB, SJ & PJ
  • Line-Level Philippine ATC Tagging (WC158, WC160, WI010)
  • 1-Click BIR Loose-Leaf / CAS CSV Ledger Export

3. RR 6-2026 Invoicing & Form 2307

Compliant billing & accounts payable with automated withholding tax certificates.

  • Separate CWT/EWT Line-Item Isolation per RR No. 6-2026
  • Automated ORIGINAL vs. REPRINT #N Watermarks
  • Instant Printable BIR Form No. 2307 Certificate Generator
  • Auto-Posting to Sales/Purchase Journals & AR/AP Ledgers

4. Philippine Tax Engine & Relief SLSP

Zero-retyping tax return computation directly from posted general ledger entries.

  • BIR Form 2550Q (Output VAT vs. Input VAT Reconciliation)
  • BIR Form 0619-E & 1601-EQ (ATC Breakdown & QAP)
  • BIR Form 2551Q (3% Quarterly Percentage Tax for Non-VAT)
  • SLSP & SAWT Relief Schedules & .DAT File Exports

5. Industry-Specific Operational Suites

Specialized sub-ledgers tailored to 5 major Philippine business sectors.

  • Retail/E-Com: Shopee/Lazada 1% CWT & SKU Valuation
  • Construction: Progress Billing, 10% Retention & Subcon EWT
  • F&B / IT-BPO / Clinics: POS Z-Read, PEZA 0% FX & HMO/PF

6. BIR CAS Audit Trail & RBAC Security

Enterprise governance satisfying BIR RMO No. 9-2021 system inspection rules.

  • 4-Tier Role-Based Access Control (Partner, Senior, Junior, Client)
  • Immutable Append-Only System Activity & Reprint Logs
  • 1-Click BIR CAS Annex C-1 Inspection Package Generator
